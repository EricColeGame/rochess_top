## Tips to Get More Out of Each RoChess Update

- **Re-benchmark the bots.** When the engine or difficulty curve changes, a bot that used to be a comfortable win becomes a real test. Play one game against each tier after a patch.
- **Use Game Review on wins, not just losses.** Accuracy scores reveal sloppy wins that feel fine in the moment.
- **Warm up with puzzles before rated games.** The puzzle rating system is separate, so it's a low-stakes way to shake off rust.
- **Learn one opening per week.** With 3,800+ openings recognized, the book will name what you played and help you build a repertoire.
- **Check leaderboards after updates.** Category resets or new seasons are common and easy to miss.

## FAQ

**How often does RoChess get updates?**
Development is fast. The tracker recorded a new build within six hours of its snapshot, and the game was only about two weeks old at that point. Expect multiple RoChess update pushes per week during busy periods, with smaller hotfixes between larger feature drops.

**Is the RoChess update free?**
The core experience is free to play, including rated games, puzzles, and bots. Optional passes like Infinite Game Review! (49 Robux) and VIP (199 Robux) add convenience, and some passes rotate offsale between releases.

**Will a RoChess update reset my rating?**
There's no indication that ratings reset with routine patches. Rated play across nine time controls is a core feature, so ratings are typically preserved. If a seasonal reset ever happens, it would likely be announced in the game description first.

**What should I check first after a RoChess update?**
Open Game Review on a recent game and compare your accuracy score, then play one bot game at your usual tier. Those two checks tell you quickly whether engine tuning or evaluation changed.

**Does RoChess run well on low-end devices?**
Based on tracker data, average FPS sits near 60 with very low variance, and average ping is around 30 ms. Chess is a light workload, so most devices should handle it comfortably even in busier lobbies.
