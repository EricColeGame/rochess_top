### How to Redeem Codes in RoChess

1. Launch RoChess on Roblox and reach the main menu.
2. Open the **Shop** button.
3. Switch to the **Codes** tab.
4. Paste or type the code exactly as written — codes are case-sensitive.
5. Hit **Redeem** and confirm your Token balance updated.

If a code refuses to take, work through the usual suspects.

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Invalid code" | Typo or extra space | Retype manually instead of pasting |
| Code worked yesterday, fails today | Expired or hit its redemption cap | Check the server's codes channel |
| Nothing happens on Redeem | Already claimed on this account | Codes are one per account |
| Balance unchanged | Server lag | Rejoin the game and recheck |

## Getting the Most Out of the RoChess Discord Community

Joining is step one. Being useful — and getting useful answers — is a skill.

**Lurk before you post.** Read a week of general chat before asking a question that's been answered forty times. Search the server for your opening name; someone has almost certainly posted a thread about it.

**Post positions, not complaints.** "Why do I keep losing?" gets ignored. A screenshot of your accuracy breakdown with "I'm bleeding material in the middlegame around move 20" gets real advice.

**Match your practice to your time control.** The server's regulars tend to specialize, and knowing which bucket you belong to helps you find the right sparring partner.

| Time control | Typical clock | What to drill |
|---|---|---|
| Bullet | Under 3 minutes per player | Tactical reflexes, premoves, flagging |
| Blitz | Roughly 3–10 minutes | Opening speed and conversion technique |
| Rapid | 10 minutes and up | Calculation, accuracy review, deeper plans |

**Use the Trello link too.** The game page pairs Discord with a Trello board. Trello is better for roadmap-style reading; Discord is better for real-time alerts. They complement each other rather than duplicate.

**Show up for events.** Community reports suggest the developers lean on milestones — like counts, visit counts, seasonal updates — to justify a fresh code drop. Those moments are also when the server is most active and most willing to help newcomers.

## FAQ

**Do I need the RoChess Discord to play the game?**
No. RoChess is fully playable without ever opening Discord. You can queue rated matches, solve puzzles, and redeem codes you find elsewhere. The server just makes all of that faster and more social — especially when you're hunting Tokens or looking for a practice partner.

**Where do I find the RoChess Discord invite link?**
The game's Roblox page lists it alongside a Trello link, usually as an icon near the title and player count. Clicking it opens an invite you can accept in your browser or the Discord app. Avoid invite links posted by random accounts in comments — go to the source.

**Are codes shared in the RoChess Discord actually free?**
Yes. Legitimate codes cost nothing and simply require redeeming in the in-game Shop. Anything asking for your password, your Roblox cookie, or a "verification" on an external site is a scam, no matter how official it looks.

**How often does the RoChess Discord get new codes?**
Community reports point to milestone-driven drops — like counts, visit thresholds, and update launches — rather than a fixed weekly schedule. The practical move is to turn on notifications for the announcements channel so you catch them while they're still live.
